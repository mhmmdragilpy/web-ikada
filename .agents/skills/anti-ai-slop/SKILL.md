# Anti-AI Slop Guide: Rules for Distinctive & Human Web Craft

## 1. Core Mandate
Never ship a web design or UI that looks like an out-of-the-box v0 / generic AI generation. Every project must possess a distinct cultural soul, deliberate art direction, tactile feedback, and authentic human copy.

## 2. Hard Anti-Patterns to Eliminate (The "Slop List")

| AI Slop Trope | Why It Sucks | What to Do Instead |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purple/Indigo radial glow blobs** | Universal giveaway of low-effort AI sites. | Use deliberate color palettes rooted in the brand: electric volt yellow, signal orange, industrial tape, deep asphalt black, newsprint grain. |
| **Identical Bento Grid** | Every section divided into uniform 3-column cards with icons. | Asymmetrical editorial grids, horizontal filmstrips, index lists, manifesto boards, tape stickers, or sticky tabbed dossiers. |
| **Robotic Corporate Copy** | "Empower your workflow", "Seamlessly innovate", "A tapestry of stories". | Natural, conversational, culturally grounded language ("Dari tongkrongan jadi cerita", "Gak usah ribet, yang penting konsisten"). |
| **Generic Floating Pill Badges** | `[ ✨ New Feature ]` everywhere on top of headers. | Camcorder REC indicators `[ ● REC 00:14:22 ]`, duct-tape style labels, utilitarian tag badges, or raw monospace metadata stamps. |
| **Overdone Glassmorphism everywhere** | `backdrop-blur-md bg-white/5 border-white/10` stacked on everything. | High contrast borders, brutalist drop-shadows (`shadow-[4px_4px_0px_#000]`), raw paper/matte surfaces, or subtle dot grids. |

## 3. The Street & Creative Indie Aesthetic System
For creative youth, community media, and production crews:
- **Typography**: Bold condensed grotesque display titles with tight tracking (`font-black tracking-tight uppercase`), contrasted with clean monospace numbers and legible body text.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Snappy tactile transitions (150ms-200ms cubic-bezier), physical button depression effects, tab switching with instant feedback, interactive audio clicks or confetti triggers on milestones.
- **Visual Texture**: Utility lines, camera viewfinders (crosshairs `+`, frame corners `⌜ ⌝`), ticket notches, tape stamps, and raw status indicators.
- **Human Touch**: Real person names, real roles, tangible examples, filterable idea cards with real slang and tongkrongan scenarios.
