# Tongkrongan Creative UI: Design System for Youth & Indie Media

## 1. Visual Identity & Spirit
- **Motto**: "Dari tongkrongan, jadi cerita. Dari cerita, jadi konten. Dari konten, jadi karya."
- **Tone & Mood**: Authentic, energetic, unfiltered, warm community, humorous, grounded in Indonesian grassroots life (street interviews, warkop chats, futsal matches, random challenges).
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Neo-Brutalist meets Indie Production Studio. Think VHS camcorder viewfinder, street flyer typography, gaffer tape accents, vibrant volt/amber highlights on deep matte charcoal.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Asphalt Night**: `#0F1015` (Deep matte background, not pure black #000)
- **Studio Slate**: `#181A20` (Surface containers, panels)
- **Electric Volt / Neon Lime**: `#CCFF00` or `#E2F952` (High-energy primary accent)
- **Signal Amber / Cine Red**: `#FF4D00` or `#FF334B` (Recording/action indicators, badges)
- **Concrete Chalk**: `#E4E4E7` (Crisp readable typography)
- **Muted Graphite**: `#71717A` (Metadata, borders, secondary text)

## 3. UI Elements & Components
- **Production Viewfinder Header**: Camera framing corners (`┌ ┐ └ ┘`), battery meter, audio VU levels, `● REC` blinking indicator.
- **Crew Dossier Cards**: Division cards with role tags (e.g. `SHOOTING // PARIS`, `PERALATAN // TEO`, `CREATIVE // ATRIA`), member pills, and division principles in bold quotes.
- **Interactive Workflow Timeline**: Step-by-step interactive tracker showing the journey from `IDE` to `EVALUASI`.
- **Bank Ide Explorer**: Searchable and filterable ideas with category tags (Sport, Guyonan, Challenge, Review, Masyarakat, etc.) and copy-to-clipboard or bookmark action.
- **Weekly Production Schedule**: Interactive weekly day-by-day timetable (Senin: Brainstorming -> Minggu: Publishing & Evaluasi).
- **Aturan Main & Prinsip**: Direct, punchy dos and don'ts formatted like a backstage production manifesto.
